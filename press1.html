SPI-LAW APPROACHES THE FEDERAL HIGH COURT TO SEEK ACCOUNTABILITY IN THE OWNERSHIP AND VALUATION OF NIGERIA’S ELECTRICITY DISTRIBUTION COMPANIES

September 25, 2026

The Society of Public Interest Lawyers in Nigeria [SPI-LAW] is committed to promoting justice, good governance, the rule of law, accountability, and human rights through strategic public interest litigation, advocacy, research, and policy engagement. SPI-LAW focuses on issues that affect the public good, with the aim of advancing transparency and the rule of law.

On the 1st day of September, 2026, SPI-LAW filed an Originating Summons at the Federal High Court, Lagos Judicial Division, Suit No. FHC/LAG/CS/1213/2026: SOCIETY OF PUBLIC INTEREST LAWYERS IN NIGERIA v. ATTORNEY-GENERAL OF THE FEDERATION & ORS.

The Case

The suit seeks the Court’s intervention to examine the ownership structure, valuation methodology, and the privatization process of Electricity Distribution Companies across Nigeria.

The Defendants

1. The Attorney-General of the Federation

2. The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission [NERC]

3. The Bureau of Public Enterprises [BPE]

4. The 11 Electricity Distribution Companies

Questions for Determination

1. Whether the valuation and subsequent transfer of the DisCos were carried out in compliance with the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 [as amended], the Electric Power Sector Reform Act, and other extant laws;

2. Whether the privatization exercise met the constitutional and statutory thresholds of transparency, accountability, and due process;

3. Whether the present ownership and operational framework of the DisCos promotes the public good and protects consumers.

Reliefs Sought

1. A declaration on the lawfulness of the process of valuation and transfer of the DisCos;

2. An order directing an independent and transparent audit of the valuation and ownership structure of the DisCos;

3. An order directing relevant authorities to ensure due process and accountability in the power sector;

4. Such further or other orders as the Honourable Court may deem fit to make.;

SPI-LAW remains committed to its mandate of advancing the rule of law and the public interest. We will keep the public informed as proceedings in this matter continue.
