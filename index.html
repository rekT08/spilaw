SPI-LAW

ABOUT US

SOCIETY OF PUBLIC INTEREST LAWYERS IN NIGERIA [SPI-LAW] CAC NO. 9727843 was registered on the 30th day of July 2026. SPI-LAW is committed to promoting justice, good governance, the rule of law, accountability, and human rights through public interest litigation and advocacy in Nigeria.

MISSION STATEMENT

SPI-LAW is a collective of lawyers passionately committed to driving systemic change through strategic litigation, advocacy, and collaboration. We champion justice, promote the rule of law, good governance, professional ethics and integrity in the legal profession, accountability, and equity, focusing on issues that impact the public good and empower marginalized and vulnerable voices in Nigeria.

AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

1. To promote justice and protect public interest in Nigeria through law.

2. To empower lawyers to use advocacy, collaboration, and litigation as tools for social change.

3. To pursue strategic legal actions on issues affecting human rights, governance, transparency, and the rule of law.

4. To train and mentor lawyers in public interest litigation and advocacy skills.

5. To partner with individuals, organizations, communities, and institutions of government to amplify the aims and objectives.

6. To conduct research and advocate for policy reforms promoting transparency and accountability.

7. To raise awareness on key public interest issues through seminars, workshops, conferences, outreach, and dialogue.

8. To assist and collaborate with public institutions to expose corruption and lack of adherence to due process.

MEMBERSHIP

Full Membership: Open to legal practitioners called to the Nigerian Bar.

Associate Membership: Open to law students.

Honorary Members: Conferred on deserving members of the Society who have exhibited sterling and unquestionable character in public interest advocacy, integrity, and transparency.

Our Team

JOHN AIKPOKPO-MARTINS, ESQ

President

He was called to the Nigerian Bar, in 1990 and has been in active legal practice in Warri, Delta State. He brings to SPI-LAW an uncommon combination of courtroom experience, public-law insight, professional leadership and institutional credibility. His practice spans civil, criminal, commercial, constitutional, electoral, land, employment, regulatory and public-interest matters.

His public-interest practice occupies a prominent place in his professional identity. Over the years, he has used litigation and institutional advocacy to interrogate public policy, defend constitutional safeguards and promote accountable administration. This work is complemented by extensive service within the Nigerian Bar Association, where he has held leadership positions at branch, section and national levels and has participated in justice-sector reform, professional-policy development and human-rights initiatives.

As President, he provides strategic direction, professional oversight and judgment across the Organization. His defining strengths are independence of mind, advocacy discipline, institutional understanding and a sustained commitment to the rule of law.

He served as 1st Vice President of the Nigerian Bar Association from 2020 to 2022, contributing to national professional governance, institutional engagement and the representation of lawyers' interests across Nigeria. He has chaired the NBA Section on Public Interest and Development Law, helping to shape the Bar's response to questions of governance, civil liberties, public accountability and development-oriented legal reform.

He was chairman, NBA Warri Branch from 2014 to 2016. He was Vice Chairman of the said branch from 2010 to 2012. He served on the Delta State Justice Sector Reform Team. He was a member of the Independent Investigative Panel on Human Rights Violations against SARS and other units of the Nigeria Police Force, convened by the National Human Rights Commission 2020-2022. He chaired the Delta State Chapter of the Association of Professional Bodies in Nigeria from 2017 to 2019.

OLUKUNLE OGHENEOVO EDUN, SAN

Vice President

Olukunle Oghene-Ovo Edun, SAN, is a distinguished Nigerian legal practitioner with over two decades of experience in the legal profession. He was called to the Nigerian Bar in 1999 and was conferred with the rank of Senior Advocate of Nigeria (SAN) in 2024. He holds a Bachelor of Laws degree from Ogun State University, a Master of Laws degree from the University of Benin, and is also a Notary Public of Nigeria.

He has served as National Publicity Secretary (2018–2020) and National Welfare Secretary (2020–2022) of the NBA, as well as a member of its National Executive Committee and General Council of the Bar. He has also held several leadership and committee positions within the NBA Warri Branch, including Vice Chairman, Publicity Secretary, Chairman of the Public Interest Litigation Committee, and Chairman of the Pro Bono Committee. His work has consistently involved public interest advocacy, human rights, access to justice, and the development of Nigeria’s legal system.

Beyond the NBA, Mr. Edun served as Executive Director and National Coordinator of the Centre for Transparency and Defence of Human Rights, where he was involved in initiatives focused on transparency, accountability, and the protection of human rights. He has also contributed to public interest litigation and justice-sector reform through his work with the NBA Section on Public Interest and Development Law (SPIDEL), the NBA Public Interest Litigation Committee, and the NBA Human Rights Institute.

At SPI-LAW, Mr. Edun brings a combination of senior-level legal expertise, extensive experience in public interest advocacy, institutional leadership, and a strong commitment to human rights and access to justice. His experience across litigation, legal professional leadership, pro bono work, and justice-sector initiatives positions him to contribute meaningfully to SPI-LAW’s efforts in advancing legal empowerment, protecting rights, and promoting accountability within the justice system.

DR. ANNE URUEGI AGI, Ph.D.

Secretary

Dr. Anne Uruegi Agi, Ph.D., is a Nigerian legal practitioner, academic, researcher, author, and public interest advocate with approximately two decades of experience in the legal profession. She holds an LL.M in Human Rights and Corporate Legal Practice from the University of Ibadan and a Ph.D. in Intellectual Property Law from the University of Calabar, with doctoral research focused on the legal protection of innovation and technology. She also holds professional certifications in Intellectual Property Law, Space Law, and Space Policy. Her legal career began in judicial research under Honourable Justice Zainab Bulkachuwa, former President of the Court of Appeal, before she proceeded into private practice, where she gained experience in litigation, corporate practice, commercial transactions, dispute resolution, conveyancing, and legal advisory services.

Dr. Agi currently serves as a lecturer at the Faculty of Law, University of Calabar, where she teaches and mentors students in areas including Intellectual Property Law, Space Law, Criminology, and Criminal Law. She is particularly recognised for her work in intellectual property, innovation, emerging technologies, and space law and policy. She is a co-founder and trustee of the LearnSpace Foundation and serves as Company Secretary of the Africa Space Leadership Institute (ASLI). Through these platforms, she has contributed to space-law education and capacity building across Africa and has mentored University of Calabar teams in the prestigious Manfred Lachs Space Law Moot Court Competition, including teams that represented Africa at international world finals.

Beyond academia and emerging areas of law, Dr. Agi has a strong commitment to public interest advocacy, human rights, access to justice, gender equality, youth inclusion, and the protection of vulnerable persons. She is an advocate of mediation, arbitration, conciliation, and peaceful dispute resolution, and has served in various professional and leadership capacities within the Nigerian legal profession and other civic organisations. She is also an accomplished author, having written on legal, social, and space-related subjects, and is a Fellow of the Institute of Corporate and Public Administrators of Nigeria.

As Secretary of SPI-LAW, Dr. Agi brings a distinctive combination of legal practice, academic research, public interest advocacy, intellectual property expertise, emerging technology and space-law knowledge, dispute resolution skills, and mentorship. Her experience in research, education, strategic advocacy, and capacity building strengthens SPI-LAW’s ability to engage with both traditional justice issues and emerging areas of law. Most importantly, her longstanding commitment to using the law to protect vulnerable people and promote access to justice aligns closely with SPI-LAW’s mission of advancing public interest, accountability, human rights, and meaningful legal interventions for society.

ADEOLA Q’AMINA FOLARIN, ESQ

Director Of Finance

Adeola Q’Amina Folarin is a seasoned Nigerian legal practitioner with extensive experience in litigation, corporate practice, governance, compliance, and legal advisory services. She obtained her LL.B (Hons.) from Lagos State University in 2009 and was called to the Nigerian Bar in December 2010 after completing her legal education at the Nigerian Law School. She subsequently obtained an LL.M from the University of Lagos in 2019. Her professional career has included experience with established law firms and legal institutions, including the Nigerian Correctional Service, Ogodo and Ogodo Chambers, Victor Opara Chambers, and Fred Odutola Chambers. Since 2018, she has been a partner at Ochai & Odihi, where she has served as External Solicitor to banks and companies and engaged extensively in litigation, legal research, corporate governance, company secretarial practice, compliance, property law, and estate administration.

Ms. Folarin has also demonstrated considerable leadership within the Nigerian Bar Association, particularly through the NBA Ikeja Branch and its Young Lawyers Forum. Her previous roles include Welfare Secretary of the NBA Ikeja Branch (2020–2022), National Executive Committee Representative (2018–2020), Chairman of the Human Rights Committee (2016–2018), and Publicity Secretary of the Young Lawyers Forum (2014–2016). She has also served as a member of the Governing Council of the National Young Lawyers Forum, Treasurer of the NBA Ikeja Branch Multipurpose Cooperative Society, Secretary of the Chief Gani Fawehinmi Annual Lecture, and a member of several NBA Conference Planning Committees. She currently serves as Director of Finance of the Society of Public Interest Lawyers.

Her professional development extends across corporate governance, arbitration, mediation, project management, and related fields. She is a member of the Nigerian Institute of Chartered Arbitrators, a mediator with the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse, and holds professional qualifications and training in corporate governance and company secretarial practice. She is also affiliated with organisations including the International Federation of Women Lawyers (FIDA), the African Women Lawyers’ Association, and the NBA Women’s Forum. Her professional engagements have included participation in national and international forums addressing human rights, women’s rights, child protection, democratic development, and the evolving role of the legal profession.

At SPI-LAW, Ms. Folarin brings a combination of active legal practice, public interest advocacy, human rights experience, corporate governance expertise, dispute resolution skills, and professional leadership. Her experience working across litigation, corporate and regulatory matters, together with her involvement in human rights and public interest initiatives, positions her to contribute to SPI-LAW’s efforts to advance access to justice, protect fundamental rights, strengthen institutional accountability, and develop practical legal interventions for individuals and communities.

SADIYA BAHJATU SALEH, ESQ

Director Of Media & Publicity

Sadiya Bahjatu Saleh, Esq., is a Nigerian legal practitioner and public-sector professional with extensive experience in legal administration, human rights advocacy, and professional leadership. She currently serves as a Deputy Director at the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), a position she has held since 2024, following several years of service with the Bauchi State Ministry of Justice, where she rose to the rank of Deputy Director, Grade Level 15.

Her career has been distinguished by significant leadership within the legal profession, particularly in advancing the participation and representation of women in the Nigerian Bar Association. She made history as the first female Vice Chairman of the Nigerian Bar Association (NBA), Bauchi Branch, the first female Chairperson of its Human Rights Committee, and the first female Chairman of the Law Officers Association of Nigeria (LOAN), Bauchi Branch. She has also served as National Publicity Secretary of the NBA Section on Public Interest and Development Law (SPIDEL), Vice Chairperson of the NBA Women’s Forum, Bauchi Chapter, and Welfare Secretary of the International Federation of Women Lawyers (FIDA), Bauchi Branch.

Ms. Saleh brings to SPI-LAW a strong combination of public-sector legal experience, human rights advocacy, institutional leadership, and a demonstrated commitment to advancing women’s participation in the legal profession. Her experience across government, professional associations, human rights structures, and public interest initiatives provides valuable insight into the practical challenges surrounding access to justice, institutional accountability, and the protection of vulnerable groups.

Her professional contributions have also received recognition, including her selection among the 100 Most Influential Lawyers, reflecting her engagement and leadership within the Nigerian legal community. At SPI-LAW, she brings a perspective grounded in legal practice, public administration, human rights, and inclusive professional leadership, supporting the organisation’s work in strengthening access to justice and promoting rights-based legal development.
