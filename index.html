Our Team

JOHN AIKPOKPO-MARTINS

President

He was called to the Nigerian Bar, in 1990 and has been in active legal practice in Warri, Delta State. He brings to SPI-LAW an uncommon combination of courtroom experience, public-law insight, professional leadership and institutional credibility. His practice spans civil, criminal, commercial, constitutional, electoral, land, employment, regulatory and public-interest matters.

His public-interest practice occupies a prominent place in his professional identity. Over the years, he has used litigation and institutional advocacy to interrogate public policy, defend constitutional safeguards and promote accountable administration. This work is complemented by extensive service within the Nigerian Bar Association, where he has held leadership positions at branch, section and national levels and has participated in justice-sector reform, professional-policy development and human-rights initiatives.

As President, he provides strategic direction, professional oversight and judgment across the Organization. His defining strengths are independence of mind, advocacy discipline, institutional understanding and a sustained commitment to the rule of law.

He served as 1st Vice President of the Nigerian Bar Association from 2020 to 2022, contributing to national professional governance, institutional engagement and the representation of lawyers' interests across Nigeria. He has chaired the NBA Section on Public Interest and Development Law, helping to shape the Bar's response to questions of governance, civil liberties, public accountability and development-oriented legal reform.

He was chairman, NBA Warri Branch from 2014 to 2016. He was Vice Chairman of the said branch from 2010 to 2012. He served on the Delta State Justice Sector Reform Team. He was a member of the Independent Investigative Panel on Human Rights Violations against SARS and other units of the Nigeria Police Force, convened by the National Human Rights Commission 2020-2022. He chaired the Delta State Chapter of the Association of Professional Bodies in Nigeria from 2017 to 2019.

OLAJIDE AKINOLA ABIODUN, ESQ.

Director Of Research

He was called to the Nigerian Bar and has remained in active legal practice, combining private legal practice with extensive experience in litigation, public-sector legal administration, constitutional and public law, professional leadership and institutional service.

He brings to SPI-LAW a combination of courtroom experience, public-law and constitutional insight, strategic litigation, professional leadership and institutional understanding. His practice and professional experience span litigation, corporate and commercial matters, constitutional and administrative law, human rights, public-sector governance, legislative drafting, regulatory compliance and legal advisory services. His professional identity is particularly defined by his interest in public-interest litigation, institutional accountability, human rights and the rule of law, with a practical understanding of how legal principles operate within public institutions, regulatory bodies and professional organisations.

He has served the Nigerian Bar Association in several strategic capacities at Branch and national levels. He served as Chairman of the Young Lawyers Forum, NBA Ikorodu Branch; Secretary of the Branch Electoral Committee; Secretary of the Branch Disciplinary Committee; Member of the National Ethics and Disciplinary Committee, and member of several other NBA committees.

He served as Vice Chairman, NBA Ikorodu Branch, from 2024 to 2026. From 2024 to 2026, he also served as Chairman of the Body of Current and Past Vice Chairmen (BOVC) of the Nigerian Bar Association, providing leadership and coordinating initiatives among Vice Chairmen across NBA Branches.

He was also appointed Secretary of the Northern Appeal Panels on NBA Elections by the then President of the Nigerian Bar Association.
